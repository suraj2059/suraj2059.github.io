Tomato & Fenugreek Dal

A slow, comforting dal simmered with tomato and dried fenugreek leaves. One pot, mostly hands-off.

35mTotal time
4Servings
EasyDifficulty

Ingredients

Red lentils1 cup
Tomato, chopped2 medium
Dried fenugreek leaves2 tbsp
Garlic, minced3 cloves
Turmeric1/2 tsp
Ghee1 tbsp

Steps

1

Rinse the lentils and simmer with turmeric and 3 cups water until soft, about 18 minutes.

2

In a separate pan, soften the garlic in ghee, then add tomato and cook until it breaks down.

3

Stir the tomato mixture and fenugreek leaves into the lentils. Simmer 10 more minutes, salt to taste.
